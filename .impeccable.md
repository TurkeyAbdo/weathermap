## Design Context

### Users
People looking up current weather by city, address, or place; a university project for Advanced Web Technologies.

### Brand Personality
Calm, clear, exploratory. Make weather discovery approachable.

### Aesthetic Direction
User approved a light weather atlas with navy text and warm orange accents. Map-first composition, soft paper background, readable typography, restrained motion.

### Design Principles
- Keep search and map at the center of the experience.
- Distinguish sample data from live observations.
- Make loading, errors, and API setup understandable.
- Support keyboard navigation, small screens, and reduced motion.
